ONE LINE (18 words)

Pocket Chronicle is a private, prompted archive of a child's childhood for
iPhone, built by one parent.


SHORT (46 words)

Pocket Chronicle sends two prompts a day about your kid — a question, and a
small mission to photograph, film, record or do. Answers take about fifteen
seconds and are filed with the prompt that caused them, the date, and your
child's exact age. Free on iPhone.


MEDIUM (92 words)

Pocket Chronicle is a private, prompted archive of a child's childhood for
iPhone. Two prompts arrive each morning: a question about your kid, and a
small mission — a photo, a video, their voice, or something to do together.
Answering takes about fifteen seconds, and every entry is filed with the
prompt that caused it, the day it happened, and the child's exact age on that
day. Voice notes are transcribed on the phone. There are no ads, no feed and
no streaks, and the whole archive exports in one tap.


LONG (163 words)

Pocket Chronicle is a private, prompted archive of a child's childhood for
iPhone, built by one parent who kept forgetting to take pictures — and who,
on the days he remembered, could not say a year later what was happening or
why he took the shot.

Two prompts arrive each morning. One is always a question: "What word do they
mispronounce that you don't want to correct?" The other is a small mission —
a photo, a video, their voice, or something to do together. Answering takes
about fifteen seconds, one-handed, and every entry is filed with the prompt
that caused it, the day it happened, and the child's exact age on that day.

Voice notes are transcribed on the phone, so a sentence a four-year-old said
is searchable years later. Any entry can be dated back years, so the archive
runs in both directions. There are no ads on any tier, no feed, and no
streaks — and the entire archive exports in one tap, to plain files that open
without the app.
