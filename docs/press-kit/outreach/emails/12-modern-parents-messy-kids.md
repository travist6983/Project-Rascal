**Subject:** A memory box that keeps the reason for the picture

```text
Hi Stephanie,

Your index-card memory box took ninety seconds: a photo or a sentence. I'm a dad who fails at both: I forget to take pictures, and when I do remember, a year later I can't say what was happening or why. The camera roll knows what my kid looked like, not what they were like.

So I built Pocket Chronicle, an iPhone app with two prompts a day: a reason to write one thing down, and a reason to take the picture. One is always a question. Entries backdate to any day, and "Look back" walks the camera roll month by month to find months nobody wrote about, reading only dates and counts on the phone. Nothing is uploaded.
```
