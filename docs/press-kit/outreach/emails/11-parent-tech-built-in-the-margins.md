## Email

**Subject:** Another app for parents, and why I built it anyway

```text
Kathryn,

Your October post on the mental load, systems instead of trying harder, is why I'm writing. You told Simply Buckhead families don't want another app. I built one anyway.

I'm a dad who forgets to take pictures. When I remember, a year later I can't say what was happening or why I took it. The camera roll remembers what my kid looked like, not what they were like. Pocket Chronicle is the system I built instead: two prompts a day. One is a question ("Describe their laugh."). The other is a mission: a photo, a video, their voice, or something to do together. Each is filed with the prompt, the date and their exact age, on a timeline they inherit.

One notification a day at most. No streaks, no guilt copy. The other parent writes into the same archive, so remembering isn't one person's job. I'm solo too: no company, no investors.

The first 14 days are full access, no card, nothing to cancel. After that the daily question stays free forever; photos, video and voice are part of membership, $5.99 a month or $59.99 a year per household. If you like it and you'll actually use it, I'll give you lifetime access. Just tell me the email you signed up with.

App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pocketchronicle-app/id6800155810
```
