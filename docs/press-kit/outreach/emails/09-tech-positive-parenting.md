Dr. Robbins,

In April you wrote that an algorithm's job is to delay the moment you put the phone down. I built an iPhone app with the opposite job.

I'm a dad who forgets to take pictures. When I remember, a year later I can't say what was happening or why I took it. The camera roll remembers what my kid looked like, not what they were like.
