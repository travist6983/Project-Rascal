01 — Voice

A friend who noticed, not an app that tracked

Rascal writes the way a parent would talk to another parent at 9pm. Specific over sentimental. Never congratulatory, never scolding — the app has no opinion about how good a parent you are, and it never reports on what you failed to do.

02 — Color

Warm paper, one hot color, three quiet meanings

Terracotta is the only color that asks for a tap. Navy is structure and type. Periwinkle, sage and terracotta each carry one meaning and never borrow each other's.

03 — Type

Nunito carries the voice, Source Sans carries the content

Nunito 800 for anything the app says about itself — screen titles, entry titles, numbers, buttons. Source Sans 3 for everything the parent wrote. Body text never drops below 16px in-app.

04 — Space, radius, elevation

Soft corners, almost no shadow

Cards sit on paper, they don't float above it. Only three things in the app are allowed a real shadow: the tab bar, the record button, and the dark status card.
