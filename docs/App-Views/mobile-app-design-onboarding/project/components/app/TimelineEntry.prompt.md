One saved day, on the rail. The payoff object of the whole product.

```jsx
<TimelineEntry live settle date="AUG 8, 2026" age="4 years, 2 months"
  kind="voice" prompt="Ask Milo what the best part of today was." artifact="0:34 · his voice" />
```

`live` + `settle` together are for the entry that was just created — the settle
animation dropping it onto the rail is the emotional beat of onboarding. Use
`settle` on exactly one entry, once.
